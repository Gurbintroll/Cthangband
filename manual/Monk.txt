Monks are melee combatants who attack without weapons. Although they can use weapons, they get [color="blue"][url="Martial Arts"]special attacks[/url][/color] when not using them. These attacks get more powerful as the monk increases in level and the monk also increases in speed as they increase in level.

As well as their physical abilities, monks are able to use magic. They are [color="blue"][url="Wisdom"]wisdom[/url][/color] based [color="blue"][url="Magic Basics"]divine[/url][/color] spell casters who cast spells from either the [color="blue"][url="Corporeal Spells"]corporeal[/url][/color], [color="blue"][url="Tarot Spells"]tarot[/url][/color], or [color="blue"][url="Chaos Spells"]chaos[/url][/color] realm. They are not as good at spell casting as other casting classes, although they are better with the corporeal realm than either of the others.

(Spells in [i]italics[/i] are found only in rare books.)
