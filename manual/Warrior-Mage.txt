Warrior-Mages are the ultimate generalists. They are good fighters (although not as good as a [color="blue"][url="Warrior"]Warrior[/url][/color]), and versatile spell casters (although not as good as a [color="blue"][url="Mage"]Mage[/url][/color]). They pay for this versatility by having the largest experience cost to increase in level of any of the classes.

Warrior-Mages can always cast spells from the [color="blue"][url="Folk Spells"]Folk[/url][/color] realm and a second realm of their choice.

They cast these spells as an [color="blue"][url="Intelligence"]intelligence[/url][/color] based [color="blue"][url="Magic Basics"]divine[/url][/color] spell caster, and as such they choose which spells to learn from their realms when they increase in level.

(Spells in [i]italics[/i] are found only in rare books.)
