Fanatics are militant followers of a Great Old One, who are granted rewards (and occasional punishments!) by their [color="blue"][url="Patrons"]patron[/url][/color] each time they increase in level.

Although primarily melee combatants, fanatics are also granted spells from the [color="blue"][url="Chaos Spells"]Chaos[/url][/color] realm. They cast these spells as a [color="blue"][url="Wisdom"]wisdom[/url][/color] based [color="blue"][url="Magic Basics"]divine[/url][/color] spell casters, and as such they receive random spells from their realm when they increase in level.

(Spells in [i]italics[/i] are found only in rare books.)
