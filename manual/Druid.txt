Druids are [color="blue"][url="Magic Basics"]divine[/url][/color] spell casters who draw their power from nature. They are experts at casting spells from the [color="blue"][url="Nature Spells"]nature[/url][/color] realm, but are unable to cast spells from other realms.

As divine casters, druids are granted random spells when they research (at the library) and use [color="blue"][url="Wisdom"]wisdom[/url][/color] as a spell casting score. As a type of priest, druids are unable to use edged weapons unless those weapons are blessed.

(Spells in [i]italics[/i] are found only in rare books.)
