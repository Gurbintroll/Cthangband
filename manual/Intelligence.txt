[center][font_size=48][color="brown"][b]Intelligence[/b][/color][/font_size][/center]
[hr color="brown"]
Intelligence is a measure of your reasoning and problem solving ability. It is of primary importance to arcane spell casters, since they use it for spell casting, and the Power per Level, Spells per Level, Spell Failure Reduction, and Min Spell Failure scores only apply to Mages, High Mages, Warrior Mages, Cultists, and Rogues. Additionally, intelligence is used for disarming traps and using magical devices.

[b]Device[/b]
This is a bonus to your Magical Devices skill. Your base Magical Devices skill is based on your profession and level, and it gets bonuses for your ancestry and for your intelligence.

[b]Disarm[/b]
This is the bonus to your Disarm Traps skill. Your base Disarm Traps skill is based on your profession and level, and it gets bonuses from your ancestry, your dexterity, and your intelligence.

[b]Power per Level[/b]
This is the amount of power per level that you have when fully rested.

[b]Spells per Level[/b]
This is the number of spells that you can learn per level. You must go back to a library to research new spells although you do not have to do this every level. Spells that remain unresearched are carried over to the next level.

[b]Spell Failure Reduction[/b]
When casting a spell, each spell has a basic chance of failure. This chance of failure is reduced by 3% per character level and also by the amount shown here for your intelligence score.

[b]Min Spell Failure[/b]
No matter how much the chance of failure when casting a spell has been reduced, it can't be reduced below this value.
