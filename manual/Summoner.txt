Summoners are fighters who supplement their own fighting skills with the ability to summon allies.

In addition to their fighting abilities, summoners are able to cast spells from the [color="blue"][url="Tarot Spells"]Tarot[/url][/color] realm, and are able to have more summoned allies at once than any other profession.

They cast these spells as an [color="blue"][url="Intelligence"]intelligence[/url][/color] based [color="blue"][url="Magic Basics"]arcane[/url][/color] spell caster, and as such they choose which spells to learn from their realm when they increase in level.

(Spells in [i]italics[/i] are found only in rare books.)
