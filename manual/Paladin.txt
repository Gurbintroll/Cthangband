Paladins are holy warriors who serve the Great Ones. Paladins are excellent melee fighters, and supplement this with the ability to cast spells from either the [color="blue"][url="Life Spells"]life[/url][/color] or [color="blue"][url="Death Spells"]death[/url][/color] realm. They cast these spells as a [color="blue"][url="Wisdom"]wisdom[/url][/color] based [color="blue"][url="Magic Basics"]divine[/url][/color] spell casters, and as such they receive random spells from their realm when they increase in level.

(Spells in [i]italics[/i] are found only in rare books.)
