Rogues are masters of stealth, able to avoid trouble by sneaking around the dungeon without waking opponents. Although not as strong as a [color="blue"][url="Warrior"]Warrior[/url][/color] in a straight fight, they do additional damage to sleeping or fleeing opponents.

In addition to their fighting abilities, rogues are able to use a small amount of magic. They are able to cast spells from any one of the [color="blue"][url="Sorcery Spells"]Sorcery[/url][/color], [color="blue"][url="Death Spells"]Death[/url][/color], [color="blue"][url="Tarot Spells"]Tarot[/url][/color], or [color="blue"][url="Folk Spells"]Folk[/url][/color] realm.

They cast these spells as an [color="blue"][url="Intelligence"]intelligence[/url][/color] based [color="blue"][url="Magic Basics"]arcane[/url][/color] spell caster, and as such they choose which spells to learn from their realm when they increase in level.

(Spells in [i]italics[/i] are found only in rare books.)
